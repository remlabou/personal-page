Presentation

I am a PhD student at the LACL (Laboratory of Algorithmic, Complexity and Logic) under the supervision of Laura Fontanella and Julien Cervelle, working mostly on realizability in set theory but also in many other aspects mixing logic, mathematics and computer science. My thesis project is about realizability models for the axiom of choice, to have a better understanding on its computational side. My studies are mostly motivated by a deep questioning about fondations in mathematics, the philosophical questions that come with and a certain interest for programing.
